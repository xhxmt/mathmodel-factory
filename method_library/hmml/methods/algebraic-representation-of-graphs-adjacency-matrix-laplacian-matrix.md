## 核心思想

The core idea is to use matrices such as the adjacency matrix and Laplacian matrix to represent the connections and relationships between nodes in a graph.

## 典型应用

This method is widely used in various fields of mathematical modeling, including network analysis, spectral graph theory, and optimization problems.
