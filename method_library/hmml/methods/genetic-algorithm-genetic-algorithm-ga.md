## 建模方法

Genetic Algorithm (GA) is an optimization algorithm that simulates natural selection and genetic mechanisms, widely used to solve complex optimization problems.

## 核心思想

The core idea of Genetic Algorithm is to simulate the evolutionary process in nature, including selection, crossover, and mutation operations, to gradually improve the quality of solutions.

## 典型应用

Genetic Algorithm is widely used in the following types of mathematical modeling problems: Combinatorial optimization problems: such as the Traveling Salesman Problem, knapsack problem, etc. Function optimization problems: In high-dimensional complex function optimization, Genetic Algorithm can effectively avoid local optima. Hyperparameter optimization in machine learning: In model training, Genetic Algorithm can be used to optimize hyperparameter configurations. Engineering design problems: such as structural optimization and parameter tuning, where Genetic Algorithm can be used to find optimal design solutions.
