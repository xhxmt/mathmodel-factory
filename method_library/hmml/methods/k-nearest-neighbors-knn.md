## 建模方法

K-Nearest Neighbors (KNN) is a non-parametric supervised learning algorithm widely used for classification and regression problems.

## 核心思想

The basic idea of KNN is to classify or predict a sample by calculating its distance to all samples in the training set, selecting the K nearest neighbors, and determining the class or predicted value based on these neighbors.

## 典型应用

KNN is widely used in the following fields: Classification problems: such as handwritten digit recognition and text classification. Regression problems: such as house price prediction and stock price prediction. Recommendation systems: recommending items similar users liked based on historical behavior.
