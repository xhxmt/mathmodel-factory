## 建模方法

Multi-level Programming is a mathematical optimization method that deals with hierarchical decision-making problems where decision-makers at different levels have different objectives and constraints.

## 核心思想

It models the interactions and constraints among decision-makers at different levels to find the optimal solution for the overall system.

## 典型应用

Multi-level Programming is widely used in economics, management, and engineering design, particularly suitable for describing and solving complex decision-making problems with hierarchical relationships.
